Millions of years ago, a sperm whale relative with teeth longer than your forearm hunted the warm seas off Peru. Meet Livyatan melvillei, one of the most formidable predators the ocean has ever produced: how big it was, what its giant teeth were for, what it hunted, and why it disappeared.
