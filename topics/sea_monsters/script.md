## Champ
[SHOT: Lake Champlain on a summer afternoon in 1977, a family on the grassy shore, calm blue water | overlay=1. CHAMP]
Number one: Champ. On July 5, 1977, Sandra Mansi was spending time with her family near Lake Champlain when something unusual happened offshore. [VERIFY: date and place]

[SHOT: Calm lake water suddenly churning violently about 150 feet offshore]
Around 150 feet away, the normally calm water suddenly started moving violently. First, a large hump appeared above the surface.

[SHOT: A long neck with a small head slowly rising out of the lake water, seen from the shore]
Then something resembling a long neck slowly emerged, ending in a small head.

[SHOT: A woman's hands holding a 1970s pocket film camera aimed at the lake]
Mansi quickly pulled out her Kodak Instamatic camera and managed to capture a single photograph before her family left the area. That photograph would eventually become the most famous piece of evidence associated with a mysterious creature known as Champ. [VERIFY: camera model]

[SHOT: A 19th-century sheriff with a lantern on a rocky lakeshore at dusk, a huge serpent shape in the water | overlay=1883]
But reports of this animal existed long before that photograph. Back in 1883, Clinton County Sheriff Nathan Mooney claimed to have witnessed a massive water serpent measuring somewhere between 25 and 35 feet. [VERIFY: 1883 Mooney sighting]

[SHOT: A vintage 1870s circus-style reward poster nailed to a wooden post by a lake, no readable text | overlay=$50,000 REWARD]
A decade earlier, in 1873, the famous showman P.T. Barnum had even announced a reward of $50,000 for anyone who could capture the creature. [VERIFY: Barnum reward]

[SHOT: Aerial view of Lake Champlain stretching between mountains, deep blue water | overlay=120 MILES LONG]
And the lake itself makes the mystery even more interesting. Lake Champlain stretches approximately 120 miles and reaches depths of nearly 400 feet. [VERIFY: length and depth]

[SHOT: Two fishermen in a small boat on Lake Champlain in 2005, fishing rods out, morning light | overlay=JULY 11, 2005]
Then, on July 11, 2005, two fishermen named Dick Affolter and Pete Bodette were out searching for salmon when they noticed something unusual floating near the surface. Initially, they assumed it was just a piece of driftwood. [VERIFY: 2005 footage]

[SHOT: A long dark shape moving through lake water in a winding snake-like motion near a boat]
But then the object started moving. Its body appeared to travel through the water in a winding, snake-like motion. Affolter later described seeing a visible section of its back measuring roughly 10 to 13 feet, along with an unusually shaped head.

[SHOT: Ripples spreading on the lake where something just dove under, a boat in the foreground]
The mysterious object suddenly disappeared beneath the surface. About an hour later, it appeared again, and this time Bodette managed to capture footage from their boat.

[SHOT: Two forensic analysts studying a grainy video frame of a lake on monitors in a dark lab]
The video was eventually examined by two retired FBI forensic image analysts. They found no obvious evidence of fabrication or digital manipulation. However, they couldn't determine exactly what the footage showed. [VERIFY: FBI analysts]

[SHOT: An underwater microphone hanging in murky green lake water, sound waves visualized]
There was another strange discovery. In 2003, researchers had recorded unusual sequences of underwater clicking sounds inside the lake. These sounds were interpreted as possible biological sonar, resembling the echolocation used by certain marine animals. [VERIFY: 2003 recordings]

[SHOT: The dark depths of a lake, faint light from above, an unknown silhouette far below]
At the time, no known native animal in Lake Champlain had been documented using this kind of echolocation. The lake contains approximately 6.8 trillion gallons of water and more than 70 islands. [VERIFY: volume and islands]

[SHOT: Lake Champlain at night under moonlight, a long neck silhouette far out on the water]
Champ has never been officially identified by scientists. But between the photographs, recordings, underwater sounds, and generations of eyewitness reports, the mystery refuses to disappear. And somewhere beneath those 400 feet of dark water, the legend of Champ continues.

## The Julia Beast
[SHOT: Looking down into the black Antarctic Ocean from an ice edge, an enormous shape moving far below | overlay=2. THE JULIA BEAST]
Number two: the Julia Beast. Imagine looking down into the Antarctic Ocean and seeing something enormous moving beneath the darkness.

[SHOT: A gigantic sea creature head with three pairs of eyes emerging from black water, dark glowing markings on its skin]
A gigantic head emerges first, followed by three pairs of eyes that appear to be looking in different directions. Its body is covered in strange dark markings, almost as if they're glowing against the black water.

[SHOT: An impossibly long dark body passing beneath a tiny research ship, no tail in sight]
The creature continues passing by, but its tail is nowhere in sight. This sounds like something from a science fiction movie. But the recording connected to this mystery is completely real.

[SHOT: An underwater hydrophone array on a cable in the deep blue Southern Ocean | overlay=MARCH 1, 1999]
On March 1, 1999, underwater listening equipment in the Southern Ocean detected a powerful low-frequency sound. The signal was eventually named Julia. [VERIFY: date]

[SHOT: A map-like aerial view of the Antarctic Peninsula and icy sea, cold blue tones]
Its source was traced to an area of the Antarctic Ocean between the Bransfield Strait and Cape Adare. [VERIFY: location]

[SHOT: A glowing audio spectrogram on a scientist's screen in a dim lab, sped-up waveform | overlay=16X SPEED]
The sound was so unusually low that the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration released an accelerated version, playing it at 16 times its original speed. And that's where the mystery begins. [VERIFY: 16x speed-up]

[SHOT: A colossal sea monster silhouette dwarfing a blue whale in the deep ocean | overlay=2,624 FT]
Over time, the Julia recording inspired stories about an unbelievably enormous sea creature, with some fictional descriptions claiming it could reach 2,624 feet in length. That's nearly 27 times the length of a blue whale measuring 98 feet.

[SHOT: A giant sea creature standing vertically beside the Empire State Building for scale, dramatic sky]
If such a creature stood vertically, it would extend more than one and a half times the total height of the Empire State Building, including its antenna.

[SHOT: A massive creature fin rotating in deep water, the body turning in a slow arc]
According to these stories, Julia has massive fins capable of rotating 180 degrees, allowing its enormous body to change direction underwater. Some fictional versions even describe Julia as a rival of another famous sea monster known as Blue.

[SHOT: Two titanic sea monsters circling each other in the abyss, faint light from far above]
Because of its supposed size, Julia is often portrayed as one of the most powerful creatures in the ocean. Its enormous body is even imagined to contain enough meat to feed an entire country for years.

[SHOT: A huge grounded iceberg grinding against the Antarctic seabed, cracks and debris in the water]
But here's the actual explanation. Scientists later connected the original Julia sound to a massive piece of Antarctic ice, most likely an iceberg that had become grounded. As the ice moved and interacted with the surrounding environment, it produced unusual sounds that traveled through the ocean. [VERIFY: iceberg explanation]

[SHOT: A lone iceberg at twilight on a calm Antarctic sea]
So, there is no scientific evidence that a giant animal was responsible for the recording. Still, the name Julia survived. And what started as a strange underwater sound eventually inspired the legend of a creature supposedly larger than anything else living beneath the ocean.

## Titanoboa
[SHOT: A steaming tropical river in an ancient Paleocene rainforest, 60 million years ago | overlay=3. TITANOBOA]
Number three: Titanoboa. Around 60 million years ago, the rivers of what is now Colombia were home to a snake so enormous that even today's largest snakes would look tiny beside it. [VERIFY: age and location]

[SHOT: A colossal snake gliding through murky jungle river water, only its huge coils visible]
Its name was Titanoboa. And unlike the other mysterious creatures on this list, this one was absolutely real.

[SHOT: A giant snake stretched along a riverbank next to a human silhouette for scale | overlay=48 FT]
Titanoboa could reach lengths of more than 46 feet, with some estimates suggesting it may have grown close to 48 feet. Its body was almost three feet thick and its estimated weight exceeded 2,200 pounds. [VERIFY: length, girth, weight]

[SHOT: An ancient swamp full of huge fish, giant turtles and crocodile-like reptiles]
It lived in a warm, humid environment filled with enormous fish, turtles, and crocodile relatives. Some of those crocodile-like predators reached around 20 feet in length. But Titanoboa was even more impressive and occupied the top of its ancient ecosystem.

[SHOT: A modern green anaconda beside a far larger Titanoboa outline for comparison | overlay=ANACONDA 23 FT]
For comparison, today's largest living snakes, green anacondas, generally reach lengths of around 20 to 23 feet. Titanoboa could grow to more than twice that length. Some reconstructions make it look almost as tall as a five-story building if its body were somehow positioned vertically.

[SHOT: A coal mine in Colombia with huge open terraces, fossil hunters working | overlay=CERREJÓN MINE, 2007]
The discovery of this giant snake began with an unusual fossil. In 2007, researcher Alex Hastings was examining fossil material recovered from Colombia's Cerrejón coal mine. [VERIFY: Hastings 2007]

[SHOT: A wooden museum box labeled with a paper tag, a giant fossil vertebra inside]
One collection box was labeled as containing crocodile remains. But inside was an enormous vertebra that didn't seem to belong to any crocodile.

[SHOT: Two scientists comparing a tiny modern anaconda vertebra with a gigantic fossil vertebra on a lab bench]
Hastings showed the bone to reptile specialist Jason Bourke. Bourke brought out a vertebra from a modern anaconda and compared the two. The similarities were remarkable. The giant fossil actually belonged to a snake.

[SHOT: Rows of fossil drawers in a museum storage room, dim light]
Even more surprising was that some of these enormous bones had already been sitting in museum collections for years. Researchers had simply assumed they were too large to belong to a snake.

[SHOT: Dozens of giant snake vertebrae laid out in a row on a long table | overlay=28 INDIVIDUALS]
Once scientists realized what they were looking at, they examined the fossil material much more carefully. Eventually, they recovered approximately 100 vertebrae belonging to 28 different individuals. [VERIFY: counts]

[SHOT: A massive snake skull with closely packed teeth, museum lighting]
Later discoveries included sections of the skull and jaws. The animal's head measured more than two feet in length, and its teeth were arranged more closely together than those of modern boas. This arrangement may have helped it grip slippery prey, including fish.

[SHOT: A short-snouted crocodile relative crushing a turtle shell in a swamp]
The surrounding fossil beds revealed an entire ecosystem of giant animals. Scientists discovered fish measuring around two and a half feet, enormous turtles, and a crocodile relative called Anthracosuchus balrogus. This predator had a short snout and powerful jaws capable of crushing thick shells.

[SHOT: Titanoboa coiled in an ancient river at sunset, mist rising]
Today, Titanoboa exists only through its fossil remains. But those enormous vertebrae were enough to reveal something extraordinary. Millions of years ago, the planet was home to a snake unlike anything alive today.

## Ningen
[SHOT: A Japanese research ship in freezing Antarctic waters at night, searchlight on the sea | overlay=4. NINGEN]
Number four: the Ningen. Far below the freezing waters surrounding Antarctica, there is a legend about something that looks strangely human.

[SHOT: A massive pale white figure moving beside a ship in dark icy water at night]
According to the stories, Japanese research crews once spotted a massive white figure moving near their vessel. At first, they thought they were looking at an iceberg or perhaps a whale. But then the figure began moving alongside the ship.

[SHOT: A giant humanoid white creature beneath the surface, long arms, human-like legs]
As it shifted beneath the surface, its shape became easier to distinguish. It appeared to have a huge torso, two long arms, and legs resembling those of a human. Its entire body was reportedly covered in completely white skin.

[SHOT: Close view of a pale featureless face in dark water with two eyes and a wide mouth, a long five-fingered hand]
The face was described as having only two visible eyes and a wide mouth, while its hands ended in five long fingers. This mysterious creature became known as the Ningen.

[SHOT: A white giant beside a blue whale in Antarctic water for scale | overlay=65–100 FT]
According to different versions of the legend, it can measure anywhere between 65 and 100 feet long. At the upper end of that estimate, it would be nearly as long as some of the largest Antarctic blue whales, which can reach approximately 110 feet.

[SHOT: A pale creature with a human upper body and a massive whale-like tail in dark water]
But unlike a whale, the Ningen supposedly has a body shaped like an enormous human. Some accounts describe it with two legs, while others give it a massive tail instead. In those versions, the creature looks like a strange combination of a giant human and a marine animal.

[SHOT: Moonlit Antarctic ice field with a pale head barely above the water, body faintly visible below]
Its supposed home is the extremely cold ocean surrounding Antarctica. Witnesses claim that it usually appears at night and spends very little time near the surface. Its pale body supposedly blends almost perfectly with the surrounding ice and water. Sometimes, only its head is visible above the ocean, while the rest of its enormous figure can be seen beneath the surface.

[SHOT: A dim room glowing with a computer monitor showing an old internet forum, rain on the window | overlay=NINGEN = HUMAN]
Even the name has an interesting meaning. In Japanese, the word Ningen simply means human. The legend gained popularity across Japanese internet communities during the 2000s. Stories circulated about mysterious sightings involving crews aboard Japanese research and whaling vessels.

[SHOT: A painted illustration on an easel showing a white giant next to a ship and icebergs]
Eventually, artists began creating illustrations showing the creature beside ships, icebergs, and frozen Antarctic landscapes. However, there is an important detail. No verified scientific evidence has ever established that the Ningen actually exists.

[SHOT: A pale silhouette sinking into black freezing water beneath ice]
Its sightings remain part of modern internet folklore rather than confirmed zoological discoveries. And according to the stories, once the enormous white silhouette disappears beneath the freezing water, there's almost no chance of seeing it again.

## Leviathan
[SHOT: A gigantic armored sea serpent rising from a stormy ocean, waves crashing | overlay=5. LEVIATHAN]
And number five: Leviathan. A gigantic shape rises from the ocean. Its back is covered with rows of tightly packed scales, resembling thousands of overlapping shields.

[SHOT: Close view of a sea monster's head breathing smoke from its nostrils and fire from its mouth over the waves]
Smoke pours from its nostrils. Flames and sparks emerge from its mouth, while the water around its enormous body turns into violent waves.

[SHOT: An ancient parchment scroll by candlelight in a stone room, a serpent drawing in the margin | overlay=BOOK OF JOB]
This is one of the most terrifying descriptions of Leviathan, a legendary sea monster mentioned in the ancient biblical Book of Job. According to the text, its body is protected by incredibly strong scales that fit together so tightly that nothing can pass between them. [VERIFY: Job 41]

[SHOT: Broken swords, spears and arrows bouncing off massive dark scales]
Swords, spears, and arrows are practically useless against it. Iron is compared to straw, while its powerful underside is described as resembling sharp pieces of pottery scraping across the seabed.

[SHOT: A multi-headed sea serpent rising from dark water under storm clouds | overlay=PSALM 74]
But Leviathan doesn't have just one consistent appearance. Different ancient texts describe it in different ways. In Psalm 74, the creature is associated with multiple heads. Meanwhile, the Book of Isaiah describes it as a twisting, fleeing serpent and a powerful monster living in the sea. [VERIFY: Psalm 74, Isaiah 27]

[SHOT: Ancient clay tablets with cuneiform writing in a museum case, warm light | overlay=LOTAN]
Interestingly, a remarkably similar creature appears in even older ancient Near Eastern mythology. Its name was Lotan.

[SHOT: A storm god with a raised club battling a seven-headed sea serpent amid lightning]
Ancient Ugaritic texts describe Lotan as a twisting, seven-headed sea serpent that was defeated by the storm god Baal. The similarities between Lotan and Leviathan are so strong that historians and scholars frequently examine the two figures together. [VERIFY: Lotan and Baal]

[SHOT: An ancient Near Eastern coastline at sunset with stone ruins overlooking the sea]
These connections suggest that the tradition of enormous supernatural sea serpents existed in the ancient Near East long before Leviathan became famous through biblical literature.

[SHOT: A sea serpent playing peacefully among old wooden sailing ships on a calm sunny sea | overlay=PSALM 104]
But there's another interesting detail. Not every biblical reference portrays Leviathan as a terrifying enemy. In Psalm 104, the creature is described as an animal created by God, playing freely in the enormous ocean among ships. This is very different from its portrayal in the Book of Job, where humans are practically powerless against it.

[SHOT: The ocean boiling and foaming around a vast armored body moving underneath]
Its chest is described as hard as stone. Weapons cannot penetrate its body. And whenever it moves, the surrounding waters appear to boil.

[SHOT: A colossal multi-headed armored serpent emerging from the depths, epic scale]
Interestingly, ancient descriptions never provide one universally accepted measurement for Leviathan. There is no confirmed length, weight, or even an agreed number of heads. But when all these descriptions are combined, they create the image of an enormous armored serpent emerging from the depths, with multiple terrifying heads and a body powerful enough to dominate the ocean.

[SHOT: Leviathan silhouette against a blood-red sunset over the sea | overlay=3,000+ YEARS]
For more than 3,000 years, the legend of Leviathan has survived through religious texts, mythology, and storytelling. And even today, its name remains one of the most recognizable symbols of a terrifying sea monster.
