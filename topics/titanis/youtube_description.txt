A few million years ago, a flightless bird taller than a man's shoulders stalked the grasslands of Florida and Texas. Meet Titanis walleri, North America's terror bird: how big it was, what its giant hooked beak was for, where its fossils were found, and why scientists still argue about whether it hunted or scavenged.
